# Lauretta Stocks

Straight from the bullpen, a team to help learn stocks and work your investment portfolio. 
Tell it how you invest give it rules, and schedule jobs to keep up to date with what matters to you. 

Introducing, your team!

- **The Director** the general operator who manages your team and directs the team.
- **Andy, the Analyst**, does the research, and helps gather facts on investments.
- **Charlie, the Checker**, trusts nobody and fact checks everything.
- **Sammy, the Strategist**, sets up a strategy to meet your risk tollerance.


## TLDR

Ask for access, and we'll set you up with an app (desktop, phone) or web access.


## Details

- **Needs:** Docker, [uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/) and [just](https://just.systems). Run
  `just` to see every command.
- **Hardware:** on a DGX Spark over Tailscale with gpt-oss-120b served with vLLM.
- **Client:** use AnythingLLM app (android, desktop) or connect over web. Voice included!
